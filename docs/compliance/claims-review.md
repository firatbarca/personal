# Claims and disclosures review

Last reviewed: 28 August 2026

## Implemented controls

- Qualified absolute statements such as “100% private,” “never makes things up,”
  “production-ready” and “reliable forecasts.”
- Explained that local/offline privacy depends on the selected model and setup.
- Explained that AI output and forecasts require human/professional review.
- Added a general-information notice to all blog posts covering legal, financial,
  assurance and investment reliance.
- Kept the Mr.Fiba earnings disclaimer and clarified that future affiliate links
  may earn a commission at no added cost to the visitor.
- Clarified that submitting a consultation enquiry neither forms a professional
  relationship nor permits confidential information to be sent.

## Publication control

- Draft posts must remain `draft: true` until all time-sensitive legal, regulatory,
  scientific and numerical claims have been checked against current primary
  sources on the intended publication date.
- A disclaimer does not cure an inaccurate claim. Record the source URL, document
  version/date and reviewer for material claims.
- Recheck already published regulatory posts when a cited rule, deadline, standard
  or delegated act changes.
- Distinguish personal commentary from statements about mandatory requirements.
- Do not describe consultation outcomes, forecasts, AI output, income or product
  readiness as guaranteed.

## Affiliate rule

Before adding an affiliate link, place a clear disclosure close to the link or at
the start of the relevant content—not only in the site footer. Identify the
commercial relationship in plain language. No product sale or checkout currently
occurs on this website.

## Sustainability rule

Avoid broad environmental claims such as “green,” “carbon neutral,” “sustainable”
or “zero impact” unless the claim has a defined scope, method, time period and
current supporting evidence. Keep evidence for comparisons and quantified claims.
