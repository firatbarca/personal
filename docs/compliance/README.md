# Website compliance working file

Last reviewed: 28 August 2026

These records support the operation of a personal and professional website that
accepts consultation enquiries but does not sell products, take payments, book
appointments, or form consulting contracts online. They are operational records,
not a legal opinion or a guarantee of compliance in every jurisdiction.

- `implementation-queue.md`: completed code work and remaining account actions
- `processor-register.md`: services, purposes and data categories
- `dpa-register.md`: processor-contract status
- `image-licence-register.md`: image provenance and gaps
- `claims-review.md`: marketing, affiliate and editorial claim controls
- `security-checklist.md`: MFA and account-security evidence checklist

Review these records at least annually and whenever a form, newsletter, analytics
tool, host, font, counter, affiliate link, or commercial service changes.
