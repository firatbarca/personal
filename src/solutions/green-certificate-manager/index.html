The challenge

Different instruments. The same need for clear evidence.

A certificate, tariff arrangement, or power purchase agreement needs context: how much energy it covers, how much has been used, what remains, when it applies, and which documents support the record.

When those details sit across spreadsheets, inboxes, and folders, a simple reporting question can turn into a search. Contributors repeat work, and reviewers spend time piecing the evidence together.

A connected record makes that context easier to find.

What it helps you do

Keep the details connected.

A focused workspace for environmental certificates and energy agreements, with the context you need for reporting and review.

Bring different record types together
Manage Guarantees of Origin (GOs), Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs), green tariff documentation, Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs), and other environmental records. Keep each type’s evidence connected to its entry.
See coverage, usage, and what remains
Track the energy covered by an instrument in kWh, the consumption used against it, and the remaining balance. Keep issue and expiry dates, covered entities, and excluded properties alongside those amounts.
Find the supporting evidence
Attach evidence files to the certificate entry, so the documentation stays connected to the information it supports.
Give contributors appropriate access
Users work with assigned records while administrative roles provide broader oversight. The company hierarchy and access arrangements can be adapted to reflect how your organisation operates.
Prepare records for review
Search and filter entries, then export a single record or the full dataset for reporting and further analysis.
Keep historical evidence available
Automated archiving preserves historical entries and supports traceability over time.
The workflow

A record you can return to.

  1. Record

    Record the certificate or agreement type, its coverage, and the relevant organisational units.

  2. Attach

    Add the supporting evidence to the same entry.

  3. Review

    Review usage against each instrument’s coverage and check the remaining balance and validity. Find and update records within your access rights.

  4. Export

    Retrieve an individual entry or the full dataset for your reporting workflow.
