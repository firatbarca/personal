The starting point

Useful writing starts
with useful material.

Your background documents often contain the detail a good draft needs. Finding the right passage and turning it into concise writing takes time.

Unicorn Writing Assistant uses relevant passages from your own files as context for drafting, helping you move from reading to writing.

What it helps you do

Built around
the work at hand.

Bring your documents into the work
Use PDF, TXT, and Markdown files as the source material for a writing task.
Find relevant context
Retrieve passages related to your request so the draft starts with material connected to the subject.
Draft across everyday formats
Prepare a report section, a short brief, or an email, then refine the structure and wording.
Keep the style consistent
Use style guidance to help identify drift in tone and keep the writing aligned with your intended voice.
Notice gaps in support
The assistant is designed to flag when supporting material cannot be found. Review those gaps and check the original sources.
The workflow

A few clear steps.
A place to begin.

  1. Load

    Bring in the documents relevant to your writing task.

  2. Ask

    Describe the subject, format, and intended audience.

  3. Draft

    Use the retrieved material as context for a first version.

  4. Review

    Check the claims against your sources and refine the final wording.

Before you begin

A local setup,
chosen with care.

The assistant is designed to run on your own machine with a compatible local model. Offline operation depends on the selected model and configuration.

We can discuss model compatibility, document handling, and your workflow before use. Generated text still needs a source check and your editorial review.
