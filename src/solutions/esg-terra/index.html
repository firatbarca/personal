The challenge

Planning needs a view beyond the last report.

Historical reports show how performance has changed. Planning adds another question: where might a metric go next, and how uncertain is that outlook?

Answering it often means checking spreadsheet inputs, selecting a forecasting method, and rebuilding charts for different audiences.

ESG Terra brings those tasks into a repeatable desktop workflow.

What it helps you do

More context for every forecast.

Explore safety and workforce metrics with data checks and reporting tools designed around the questions practitioners ask.

Check the data before forecasting
Review missing information, inconsistent records, and relationships between KPIs. Checks cover workforce figures, injury and ill-health records, fatalities, and absence data.
Compare models automatically
Auto mode tests candidate forecasting models against historical data and selects the option with the lowest error among those tested.
Choose the view your question needs
Explore group, division, country, region, or entity views. Choose a forecast horizon from 30 days to 12 months.
Keep uncertainty in the conversation
Review projected trends alongside uncertainty intervals and model diagnostics. Use the results to support professional judgement about the outlook.
Prepare the next reporting conversation
Export forecast charts and supporting commentary to PowerPoint. Export data quality findings to Excel for further review.
The workflow

A clear path through the analysis.

  1. Load

    Import a CSV or Excel file. Use the available template to structure your data.

  2. Check

    Run the data quality checks and review flagged issues before forecasting.

  3. Forecast

    Select a KPI, organisational scope, and horizon. Run the analysis.

  4. Review & export

    Examine the outlook and its uncertainty, then export the results for discussion.

The approach

Domain knowledge, built into the workflow.

Created by Firat Barca, an ESG practitioner, ESG Terra connects analytical work with the reporting that follows. Its checks reflect relationships within safety and workforce data, while its exports help carry the analysis into management discussions.

A desktop starting point

ESG Terra is documented as a desktop application that imports CSV and Excel files. That provides a familiar starting point for teams working with their own datasets.

Data storage, external connections, and offline availability need to be confirmed for the supplied configuration. Desktop operation alone does not establish that data stays entirely on the device.

Adaptable to your needs

Different teams.

Teams work with different data and reporting requirements. ESG Terra can be adapted to your needs, with the scope assessed against your workflow.

We can discuss changes to input fields, organisational views, reporting formats, access arrangements, or integrations. Analytical requirements can also be reviewed. Any adaptation needs an agreed scope and feasibility review.
