The challenge

You have ideas.
Give them a starting point.

Between finding an angle, gathering sources, and shaping the words, even a short professional post can take time.

Pantomime Intelligence brings those steps into a connected workspace. Your interests set the direction; documents and research provide context; examples of your writing help guide the tone.

The draft is a starting point for your judgement and experience.

What it helps you do

Build a post around
something worth saying.

Move between ideas, source material, and writing without starting each time from an empty page.

Find your angle
Use your role, keywords, and content pillars to steer the subject. Choose a format, length, and voice to suit the post.
Bring your own material
Add PDFs and examples of your writing to the Library. Use document context and familiar phrasing to help shape the output.
Explore what’s current
The Newsroom surfaces headlines around your keywords, while the Research Desk and academic search help you explore papers. Pin material to return to it.
Keep your voice in the process
Voice examples guide the writing style. Edit the result and add a concrete detail from your own work before sharing.
Review and retain your drafts
Revise the text, check claims flagged for verification, and use the available save and export options to keep useful drafts.
Keep up with your interests
Optional email digests provide news or research summaries based on your keywords, giving you material to consider for future posts.
The studio workflow

Gather. Shape.
Review. Share.

  1. Gather

    Set your role and keywords. Add documents, voice examples, and relevant research.

  2. Shape

    Pick a content pillar, format, length, and tone. Give the studio a direction for the draft.

  3. Review

    Check source support and flagged claims. Refine the words and add your own experience.

  4. Share

    Take your finished personal post to LinkedIn. The Studio leaves that publishing step with you.

For company-page workflows

Plan the work
around the post.

The separate Engagement area adds queue, board, and calendar views for company-page content, alongside idea generation and an approval step before publishing.

This is a different workflow from personal drafting in the Studio. Its availability depends on your account, plan, and connection setup; check the app for current access.
