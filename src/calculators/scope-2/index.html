Which factor is location based?

If it describes the average electricity grid where you consume electricity, it is generally a location based factor.

Which factor is market based?

If it comes from qualifying supplier or contractual electricity information, it may be used for market based reporting.

Teaching example

A. Company Scope 2 inventory

GreenOffice Ltd. buys 100,000 kWh of electricity during the year.

Electricity consumed 100,000 kWh
Grid EF 1,000 g CO₂e/kWh
Supplier EF 200 g CO₂e/kWh
Location based Scope 2 100 t CO₂e
Market based Scope 2 20 t CO₂e
100 t − 20 t = 80 t difference between the two inventory results
80 t CO₂e

This is an accounting difference. It is not automatically avoided emissions.

B. Separate solar project analysis

Now imagine GreenOffice Ltd. separately supports a new solar project that generates 30,000 kWh.

New solar generation 30,000 kWh
Simplified baseline EF 1,000 g CO₂e/kWh
Estimated emissions without the project 30 t CO₂e
Simplified project operating emissions 0 t CO₂e
Baseline: 30,000 kWh × 1,000 g CO₂e/kWh ÷ 1,000,000 = 30 t CO₂e
Avoided emissions: 30 t baseline − 0 t project operating emissions = 30 t CO₂e
30 t CO₂e

In this simplified teaching example: 30 t baseline emissions − 0 t project operating emissions = 30 t CO₂e estimated avoided emissions.

Why are 80 t and 30 t different?

The 80 t figure is the difference between two Scope 2 inventory results for the same 100,000 kWh of electricity. The 30 t figure comes from a separate solar project calculation comparing the assumed baseline without the project with the simplified project scenario. They answer different questions and should not be compared as if they were the same type of result.

Important: For simplicity, this teaching example reuses the 1,000 g CO₂e/kWh average grid factor as the project baseline and assumes 0 t of project operating emissions. This is only to explain the calculation. In a real avoided emissions study, the correct counterfactual baseline may be different from the average grid factor because the generation actually displaced by the project can have a different emissions intensity. Relevant project emissions and other significant effects should also be considered. Avoided emissions should be reported separately from the Scope 2 inventory.

What should I remember before reporting?

Do not add location based and market based results together. They are two different views of the same electricity consumption.

A supplier specific factor is not automatically valid simply because the supplier provided it. Check the applicable Scope 2 quality criteria.

A lower market based result does not by itself prove avoided emissions.
