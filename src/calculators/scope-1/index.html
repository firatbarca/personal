There is no single global database that is automatically correct for every operation.

How do I choose the right factor?

Check the geography, reporting year, fuel or activity, unit, gases included, GWP basis, intended use, and the requirements of your reporting framework. Document the source and version used.

What does refrigerant GWP mean?

Global Warming Potential compares the warming effect of a greenhouse gas with carbon dioxide over a defined time horizon. One kilogram of a refrigerant with a GWP100 of 1,430 corresponds to 1,430 kilograms of CO₂e under that GWP basis.

Why are the factors editable?

The prefilled values are useful starting points, not universal answers. A formal inventory should use the factor that fits the activity, location, reporting year, and reporting framework.
